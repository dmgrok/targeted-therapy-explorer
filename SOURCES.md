## Copyrighted text

- **PubMed abstracts** are treated as publisher (or author) copyright. Citation cards show only metadata (title, authors, journal, year, PMID link). This was already the case before this review; a gate test now locks it in.
- **FDA label excerpts** are U.S. government public text delivered by openFDA / DailyMed. Each excerpt is attributed and linked to the official label.
- **EMA** text is reproduced only as short workbook fields (name, INN, status, a short indication in a citation), with the Agency acknowledged and a link to the EPAR. Full assessment reports are not copied.
- **CIViC** text is CC0.

## Patient data

Trial records are registry-level only. Snapshot locations keep institution facility name, city and country. Trial cards show only countries plus a ClinicalTrials.gov link. No contact names, emails or phone numbers are stored or shown. A gate test fails if any appear.

## Terms that are restrictive or that could not be re-extracted this turn

- **ClinicalTrials.gov terms page** (`https://clinicaltrials.gov/about-site/terms-conditions`): a JavaScript application. A direct fetch on 12 November 2026 returned the shell (~94 KB) with almost no extractable legal text. We did not invent terms. The site continues to follow the official terms last updated 31 January 2023, already recorded on the About page: free to use, attribute ClinicalTrials.gov, display the processing date, keep current, no proprietary rights, no email marketing, international copyright outside the United States.
- **FDA website and Drugs@FDA pages**: a direct curl from this environment was blocked as an automated request. FDA website policies were retrieved with a separate fetch: U.S. government work, recommend dating the copy and linking back. We do that. The PGx and CDx tables themselves are used as public FDA web pages and are linked and dated.
- **PubMed abstracts**: restrictive if copied. We do not copy them.
- **OncoKB**: restrictive; not used.
- No other source used by the build was found to forbid this public, attributed use.
