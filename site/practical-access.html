Personalised mRNA cancer vaccines today

All are investigational.

One setting. One combination. Not an approval. Overall survival is not mature. The colorectal trial was stopped. These products are not ranked.

Intismeran autogene (V940 / mRNA-4157)

Merck Sharp & Dohme and Moderna. Investigational.

Phase 3 INTerpath-001 is NCT05933577. The link is the registry page only.

It is adjuvant intismeran plus pembrolizumab, versus pembrolizumab alone, after complete resection of stage IIB–IV cutaneous melanoma.

Source: Long et al., NEJM. Published 11 Nov 2026. Last checked 15 Nov 2026.

Long et al. published the results in the NEJM on 11 Nov 2026. MSD and Moderna funded the paper.

1,137 were randomised (757 vs 380). The written order is intismeran plus pembrolizumab, then pembrolizumab alone.

Median follow-up was 24.6 months.

24-month recurrence-free survival was 82.2% vs 70.0% (two-sided P<0.001), in that same order.

24-month distant-metastasis-free survival was 88.5% vs 80.2% (P=0.002), in that same order.

Grade ≥3 adverse events were 23.6% vs 20.3%, in that same order.

Source: Long et al., NEJM. Published 11 Nov 2026. Funded by MSD and Moderna. Last checked 15 Nov 2026.

A Merck and Moderna release of 22 Oct 2026 still calls it investigational. It says overall survival is not mature.

designed and produced using a patient's tumor sample

Merck and Moderna release, 22 Oct 2026. Release. Last checked 15 Nov 2026.

a synthetic mRNA coding for up to 34 neoantigens.

Merck and Moderna release, 22 Oct 2026. Release. Last checked 15 Nov 2026.

Source: Merck and Moderna release. 22 Oct 2026. Last checked 15 Nov 2026.

Autogene cevumeran (BNT122)

BioNTech and Genentech. Investigational.

A Phase 1 study in pancreatic cancer is in Rojas et al., Nature, 10 May 2023. Neoantigen selection in that study is bioinformatic neoantigen prediction.

bioinformatically predicted and ranked by immunogenicity

Methods. Rojas et al., Nature, 10 May 2023. Nature. Last checked 15 Nov 2026.

The same paper reports neoantigen-specific T cells in 8 of 16 patients.

Source: Rojas et al., Nature. 10 May 2023. Last checked 15 Nov 2026.

Phase 2 IMcode003, NCT05968326, is in resected pancreatic cancer. It continues. The link is the registry page only. No registry fields are copied. A registry status may lag a sponsor statement.

Checked 15 Nov 2026. No separate source link is added for the word “continues”. The registry link is the page only.
