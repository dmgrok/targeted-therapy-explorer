Jill Feldman

EGFR exon 19 lung cancer

Summary

Jill Feldman was diagnosed with lung cancer in 2009, at age 39.

The Patient Story, in a 10 January 2025 interview, lists the cancer as stage 1A, with an EGFR exon 19 deletion.

In her own words in that interview, she said she was EGFR-positive.

She wrote in June 2020 that she took erlotinib after her first surgery, even though it was not standard care.

She named her oncologist, Phil Bonomi, in that post.

She wrote that the drug worked until she stopped taking it. A new nodule appeared eight months later.

As of 10 January 2025, she described a later progression in the spring, radiation to that area, and staying on the same treatment.

She did not name that later drug in the January 2025 interview we opened.

We found no first-person update from her in 2026 about her scans or her drug.

In their own words

I was diagnosed with lung cancer in 2009 at 39 years old.

Per The Patient Story interview, 10 January 2025. Page opened 13 November 2026.

I didn’t want my kids to ever go through what I went through, so I took Erlotinib (off label) as adjuvant therapy. And it worked…. until I stopped taking it.

Per her post of 12 June 2020. Page opened 13 November 2026.

That just happened to me in the spring. I had progression, and I ended up being able to radiate the area where the cancer was growing. Now, I stay on the same treatment.

Per The Patient Story interview, 10 January 2025. Page opened 13 November 2026.
