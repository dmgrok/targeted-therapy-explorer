Janet Freeman-Daily

ROS1-positive lung cancer, crizotinib

Summary

Janet Freeman-Daily learned she had lung cancer in 2011.

She first had chemotherapy and radiation. The cancer later spread outside her chest, and then to her other lung.

On 25 September 2012, the University of Colorado told her that her tumour had a ROS1 gene change.

She was eligible for a trial of a daily pill called crizotinib.

She started that trial in November 2012 in Denver.

A University of Colorado article dated 3 June 2025 quotes her account of meeting Paul Bunn, MD, there.

This page uses her own words for how she is now.

In their own words

But on this date, the University of Colorado notified me that my tumor tissue tested positive for a gene rearrangement called ROS1, and I was eligible for a clinical trial of the targeted therapy pill crizotinib.

Per her blog post of 25 September 2025. Page opened 13 November 2026.

Today I’m still on the same daily pill.

Per her blog post of 25 September 2025. Page opened 13 November 2026.

I’ve been on the same oral therapy (with a couple of dose reductions) for over thirteen years. My scans have shown no sign of cancer since I started on that drug.

Per her blog post of 10 May 2026. Page opened 13 November 2026.
