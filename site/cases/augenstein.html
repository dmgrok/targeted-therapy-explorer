Neal Augenstein

EGFR exon 19 lung cancer, osimertinib and surgery

Summary

Neal Augenstein was diagnosed with stage 4 non-small cell lung cancer just before Thanksgiving 2022.

He wrote that biomarker testing showed an EGFR exon 19 deletion.

For that gene change, he took a daily pill called osimertinib, also known as Tagrisso.

On 31 March 2023, he wrote that the pill he had taken since December had cleared almost all visible cancer in both lungs.

He was being treated at Inova Schar Cancer Institute in Fairfax, Virginia, and hoped to have surgery.

He later had a robotic-assisted lung lobectomy.

In their own words

The biomarker testing showed I have EGFR exon 19 deletion. For that particular mutation, there’s a drug called osimertinib, with a brand name of Tagrisso.

Per his WTOP article of 31 March 2023. Page opened 13 November 2026.

it appears the one-pill-a-day targeted therapy I’ve taken since December has cleared all but the tiniest remnants of my stage 4 lung cancer

Per his WTOP article of 31 March 2023. Page opened 13 November 2026.

I’ve had no evidence of disease, and have remained cancer-free since May 2023, while continuing to take my daily pill.

Per his WTOP article of 23 November 2025. Page opened 13 November 2026.

Despite having no evidence of cancer now, I (and my doctors) wouldn’t say that I’m “cured,” since I was originally diagnosed after cancer had spread from the original site in my left lung.
