Sid Sijbrandij

Osteosarcoma of the spine

Summary

Sid Sijbrandij has bone cancer, called osteosarcoma, in the T5 vertebrae of the upper spine.

On his own cancer page, he wrote that he has taken agency in that treatment.

He was diagnosed in November 2022, according to osteosarc.com, a site that shares his data.

The primary tumour was removed from his spine in December 2022 at UCSF.

The cancer came back at the Th4 vertebra in June 2024, at UCLA.

It came back again at Th4 to Th5 in January 2025, also at UCLA, and was removed in April 2025 at MSKCC.

On his cancer page, he wrote that he had run out of standard treatment options, and that no trials were available for him.

He then started what he calls maximum diagnostics, created new treatments, used more than one treatment at a time, and began to scale this for others.

In a 20 January 2026 post, he looked back at late 2024. He said his doctors told him he had exhausted the standard of care. He said there were no trials for his situation.

He called that path Founder Mode. This page does not present that path as advice, and it does not say it works for anyone else.

His cancer page links to a recording of an OpenAI Forum talk. The pages we opened do not spell out, in his own words, how he used any AI tool, so this page does not describe that.

In their own words
